ROV Thruster Mount

Designed for the Memorial University Eastern Edge Robotics Team for the 2026 MATE ROV World Championship.

Onshape 3D Printing Mechanical Design Design Iteration Assembly
💡 IDEA
  • Design a lightweight thruster mount for the ROV.
  • Improve strength and reliability.
  • Create a design that could be easily manufactured with 3D printing.
⚙ PROCESS
  • Created sketches and initial concepts.
  • Designed multiple versions in Onshape.
  • 3D printed and tested prototypes.
  • Modified the design based on testing and team feedback.
  • Installed the final part onto the competition ROV.
✔ RESULTS
  • Final mount successfully installed.
  • Improved durability compared to earlier concepts.
  • Used during the 2026 MATE ROV World Championship.
  • Gained valuable experience in CAD and design iteration.
