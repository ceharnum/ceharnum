BlueStar – Corner Thruster Mounts

Designed custom corner thruster mounts for the BlueStar ROV using Onshape and 3D printing.

Idea

  • Design and fabricate 45° corner mounts to support multiple Blue Robotics T200 thrusters on the BlueStar ROV.
  • Create a lightweight yet durable mounting system capable of withstanding thrust forces during operation.
  • Ensure the mounts integrate securely with the ROV chassis while allowing accurate thruster positioning.

Process

  • Designed multiple iterations of the thruster mounts using Onshape CAD.
  • Fabricated prototypes on a Bambu Lab 3D printer and evaluated each design for strength and fit.
  • Mounted the final components onto the BlueStar chassis and tested them under operating conditions.

Results

  • Produced a durable final design that securely mounted all corner thrusters.
  • Successfully withstood propulsion loads during pool testing and competition preparation.
  • Used on the BlueStar ROV at the 2026 MATE ROV World Championship.

BlueStar – Chassis

Mechanical design and assembly of the BlueStar ROV chassis.

Idea

  • Design a strong, lightweight chassis capable of withstanding the challenges outlined in the 2026 MATE ROV competition.
  • Provide a rigid structure to support propulsion, tooling, and onboard electronics.
  • Maximize interior space while maintaining strength and ease of assembly.

Process

  • Researched structural materials and selected 20 × 20 mm aluminum T-slot extrusion for its strength, weight, and modularity.
  • Measured, cut, and assembled the frame using machine shop equipment, including a horizontal bandsaw.
  • Mounted and tested mechanical, electrical, and tooling systems to ensure proper fit and functionality.

Results

  • Produced a durable, lightweight chassis that supported all mechanical systems and mission tooling.
  • Provided a stable platform for integrating the ROV's electrical and mechanical components.
  • Successfully competed in the 2026 MATE ROV World Championship.

Miniature Zamboni Project

A backyard ice resurfacer concept developed during Engineering Graphics and Design course.

Idea

  • Design an affordable solution for maintaining smooth ice surfaces on backyard and pond rinks.
  • Address common challenges faced by rink owners, including snow buildup, ice damage from skaters, and frozen water hoses.
  • Create a practical concept that would improve rink maintenance while remaining accessible to recreational users.

Process

  • Researched existing ice resurfacing equipment and identified opportunities for a lower-cost alternative.
  • Collaborated with teammates to brainstorm ideas and develop design concepts.
  • Created sketches, CAD models in SolidWorks, and 3D-printed prototype components to communicate the final design.

Results

  • Developed a practical miniature Zamboni concept for recreational ice maintenance.
  • Successfully communicated the design through CAD models, prototype components, and a formal presentation.
  • Demonstrated teamwork, engineering design, and technical communication skills throughout the project.

Snake Game Programming Project

Python and Arduino game developed for Introduction to Programming course.

Idea

  • Design and develop a multi-level Snake-style video game using Python and Arduino hardware components.
  • Integrate interactive features, including joystick controls, LEDs, and a buzzer, to enhance gameplay and user experience.

Process

  • Researched and learned new programming tools and hardware components, including Python’s Turtle module and Arduino inputs/outputs.
  • Developed structured plans and flowcharts to organize game mechanics before programming the multi-level game.
  • Programmed, tested, and debugged gameplay features to improve functionality and ensure reliable operation.

Results

  • Produced a fully playable multi-level Snake-style video game integrating Python software with Arduino hardware.
  • Successfully demonstrated the completed project to classmates and visiting high school students for testing and feedback.
  • Strengthened programming, collaboration, and technical communication skills through project development and presentation.

Eastern Edge Robotics

Since September 2025, I've been a member of the Mechanical Team for Memorial University's Eastern Edge Robotics team. Each year, Eastern Edge Robotics designs, manufactures, assembles, and tests a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) to compete in the annual MATE ROV World Championship, where the competition challenges teams to solve real-world ocean engineering problems. Through this experience, I've developed hands-on skills in CAD design, machining, fabrication, assembly, testing, and teamwork while contributing to the design and construction of our competition ROV.
